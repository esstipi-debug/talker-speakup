# Design guide — SpeakUp M7 (Pronunciation Gym + Prosody Snapshot)

Panel synthesis. Four lenses, adversarially refuted; refutations win. Everything below is grounded in files I opened.

---

## 1. Blocking conflicts

**None unconditional.** Everything in the locked table is buildable as written. One *conditional* conflict must be branched now, before the spike, so the decision is arithmetic later instead of a negotiation.

### C1 — If the MFA container cannot produce a phone tier at all, D1 + D2 + D3 cannot hold in one milestone

- **Decision at risk:** D1 (teaching and measuring in one milestone), D2 (hybrid: snapshot **plus** a dedicated Gym), D3 (forced alignment).
- **Evidence:** brief R3 — the official MFA Dockerfile does `ENV MFA_ROOT_DIR=/mfa`, `RUN mfa server init`, and starts PostgreSQL only from `~/.bashrc`; a FastAPI ENTRYPOINT never sources it, so alignment dies with a DB connection error. On Windows this lands in WSL2, where PG socket/lock behaviour on bind mounts is the known failure. There is no fallback aligner — every CTC alternative is licence-excluded (brief §6: `torchaudio MMS_FA` CC-BY-NC, `ctc-forced-aligner` NC + DOSL-1.0, `charsiu` unlicensed weights, `gentle`/`aeneas` rejected). Docker Desktop is currently unreachable on this host (`npipe:////./pipe/dockerDesktopLinuxEngine`).
- **Not triggered by latency.** brief R2 already resolves that outcome: *"If a warm worker can't get under ~1.5 s, MFA is Gym-only"* — which is the architecture already chosen. A 4–10 s cold `align_one` subprocess is fine behind a "scoring…" state in a screen where the user just finished a deliberate recording. Latency decides cold-subprocess vs warm-worker and nothing else. Do **not** write a latency number into a descope clause.
- **Two options, pick now:**
  - **(a) Ship M7 as browser-only.** Slices 1–2 only: pause profile + persistence spine. The Gym, echo scoring and all alignment-derived metrics move to M8. D1/D2/D3 are explicitly deferred, in writing.
  - **(b) Timebox a rescue, then fall to (a).** One extra day on: `--temporary_directory` on tmpfs, `/mfa` on a **named volume** (never a Windows bind mount), and either PG started from the entrypoint before uvicorn, or `mfa configure --disable_auto_server` with SQLite alignment verified. If that day ends without a phone tier, take (a) without further discussion.
- **Recommendation: (b).** The rescue is well-understood and bounded; (a) is the floor either way.

| Pause **placement** without word timings | Chrome's `SpeechRecognitionAlternative` exposes only `transcript` + `confidence` — no timings (`client/src/lib/speech.js:40-52`). **Do not** interpolate character offsets from `event.timeStamp`: that is *dispatch* time, and the recognizer finalizes *because* it detected the silence, so the anchor is displaced by exactly the interval you're measuring. **Invert it** — use the recognizer's own endpoint decisions as boundary evidence. Record the worklet-clock time of each finalization. ## 3. The teaching loop, concretely

The panel's sharpest finding: **the design as presented is a measurement loop, not a teaching loop.** Model → attempt → feedback exists; re-attempt and spacing — the two steps that cause change — are undefined. RhythmTA's only *objective* result (brief §5) is that the visualisation bought **more attempts per clip (3.08 vs 2.26, p<.05, at no time cost)**; every Likert win is n=12 self-report after one session. The design imported the chart and dropped the mechanism its only hard evidence supports.

### The Gym session

Fixed size: **6 items, ~7 minutes.** 3 due (`nextDrillAt <= now`, highest `frequency` first) + 2 new (BLOCKER before POLISH, never mixed) + 1 free/user-picked. Fixed size is what makes "done" reachable. **Cold start:** with a `frequency >= 3` scheduling gate nothing is due before roughly day 4, so the set degrades to "new only" for the first week — say so in the spec.

### One item, step by step

**1 — MODEL.** Kokoro plays the pre-rendered line (`GymUnit.ttsCachePath`). Permanently visible, not as a fallback: the sentence with stressed syllables in `<strong>`, and the ELT notation `da-DA-da-da-DA` (design-so-far:39 — this is the notation ELT teachers already draw; it transfers to a human tutor and it prints). Beat karaoke (C) animates over it — words light in time, the metronome pulses on stressed beats only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the notation and the bolded sentence are the whole model step and nothing is lost. A **"Play slow"** button re-synthesises at `speed: 0.75` server-side.

> Coach copy: *"Listen for the shape: two beats, not five. da-DA-da-da-DA."*

**2 — ATTEMPT.** Learner taps record and says the line. `micStream` worklet captures raw Float32; on stop it resamples to 16 kHz mono via `OfflineAudioContext` and POSTs multipart to `/pron/score`. A `GymAttempt` row is written **before** the sidecar is called.

**3 — FEEDBACK.** Duration ribbon (B): target above (seed-time alignment of the Kokoro render), learner below, one shared time axis, word granularity by default (design-so-far:36). Below it, **one imperative sentence** from the deterministic template table, derived from the largest deviation only — capped, sorted descending, smallest dropped (brief §5, RhythmTA's own algorithm). Beside it, the hum A/B: two `<audio controls>`, target tune and your tune, words stripped.

> M3 polysyllable: *"'COMfortable' — the middle vowel is doing too much work. Squash it, lean on COM."*
> Weak form: *"'to' came out full. Let it go soft — 'tuh'."*
> Nuclear stress: *"You put the peak on MONEY. The contrast is on HE."*
> Abstained: *"Couldn't hear that clearly enough to score. Once more, a bit closer to the mic."*
> Sidecar down: *"Not scored — scorer offline."* (with a Retry button that re-runs the current attempt if the audio is still in memory)

New contrasts only, once, stored as a static `l1Anchor` string on the unit and never generated: *"la /i/ de piso, no la de sheep."* Everything else in English — at C1–C2 the instruction is itself input.

**4 — RE-ATTEMPT.** One button: **"Try again."** Unlimited. Not a mandatory 3-block — mandating three attempts on a unit you nailed first try is precisely RhythmTA's DR6 failure mode. From attempt 2 the ribbon renders **three** tracks: target (solid, always — it is a locked visual), previous attempt (ghosted 40%, dashed), current. The sentence names whichever delta is larger and says which it is:

> *"Better — that vowel came down 30 ms. Again, further."*
> *"Still 60 ms long. Try holding the first syllable instead."*

The unit closes when the learner closes it. **Never on a score threshold** — a score gate on an uncalibrated metric produces an unpassable exercise.

**5 — LEDGER WRITE.** On close, grade **attempt 1** (clean → advance, failed → reset). Upsert into `ErrorLedger` with `type = "pronunciation"`. `pattern` names a **practisable class**, never an instance; the offending word goes in `example`:

| `pattern` | Written when |
|---|---|
| `pron:reduce:{word}` | M3 ratio failure on a polysyllable |
| `pron:weakform:{word}` | M3-extended failure on a monosyllabic function word |
| `pron:lexstress:{word}` | Stress on the wrong syllable (cognate drill) |
| `pron:nuclear:{contextType}` | `contrastive` \| `given-info` \| `final-default` — three rows total, not one per sentence |
| `pron:seg:{EXPECTED}>{PRODUCED}` | Minimal-pair `'substituted'` — only if K4 passed |

Upsert on every **non-abstained** failure (`verdict !== 'unclear'` and `alignmentConfidence` above the gate); `frequency++`. A drill is only *scheduled* at `frequency >= 3` — keeps the data, de-noises the schedule. Never write `pron:reduction:{AH0}`: schwa is one near-permanent row carrying no targeting signal.

**6 — SPACING.** `nextDrillAt` on a fixed ladder: **1d → 3d → 7d → 21d** on a clean attempt-1 pass; **reset to 1d** on failure. `drillCount` increments. That is the whole scheduler — five lines, no ease-factor drift, and it survives the metric being recalibrated.

**7 — UNIT SELECTION when the sidecar is down.** Selection is **least-recently-`startedAt`**, tie-broken by worst score among rows with non-null `metrics` — *not* gated on a score. Otherwise a sidecar-down Gym serves the same unit forever and leaves no trace. No backfill queue, no retry job, no job ids: a score arriving days later attached to a forgotten rep is worth less than the code it costs. `PRON_KEEP_AUDIO=1` is a dev-only flag for the K3 calibration work, not a product feature.

### The echo moment (in conversation)

Deterministic, client-side policy in `useConversation` (the server is stateless — `runTurn(utterance, history)` has no session store and there are zero `PrismaClient` imports in `server/src`). Offer on the **first eligible reply once ≥4 turns have passed since the last offer, max 3/session** — a `turnIndex % 5` modulus silently costs you five turns whenever the reply is the wrong length. The line must come from the curated echo set, so ARPAbet is pre-resolved at seed time and OOV is impossible by construction (D7: LLM proposes conversational sentences, never scored ones).

Inline button on the last coach bubble. Never a modal, never during `speaking`/`thinking`. → stress dots (D): target row vs your row, big filled = stressed, small hollow = unstressed, mismatches ringed with an `x` glyph (size + fill, two channels — never a colour swap). One `aria-label`: *"Target rhythm: da-DA-da-da-DA. Yours: da-da-DA-da-DA. Two words differ: 'the' and 'money'."* Ledger write identical to the Gym. Sidecar down ⇒ pure modelling, target row only, no score (design-so-far:31).

### Free conversation

The pause profile accumulates **every turn, silently**. Rendered at most **once per 3 turns**, and only when the turn had ≥3 detected pauses ≥250 ms — one plain sentence, no chart, no number. It is not actionable in the next 3 seconds anyway; "move the breath to the comma" is a tomorrow instruction. Surface the accumulation at **end of session**, as the reason a unit is queued:

> *"You broke mid-phrase 11 times today. Tomorrow starts with chunking."*

### What can actually be claimed

Write this sentence in the spec, verbatim:

> *This milestone can demonstrate improvement on drilled sentences. It cannot yet demonstrate transfer to free conversation. Clause-internal pause rate per 100 words is the one number that would begin to show it.*

Two weekly numbers, both within-learner, both plotted; everything else is instrumentation. (1) **Clause-internal pause rate per 100 words** of free conversation — never per turn. (2) The **6 PROBE sentences**, re-read weekly, for the M5 VarcoV trend. `comparableToPublished` is hardcoded `false`.
